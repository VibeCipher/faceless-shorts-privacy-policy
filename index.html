Privacy Policy — Faceless Shorts Bot

Last updated: 2026

Faceless Shorts Bot is a personal automation script owned and operated solely by its developer, Soham Chatterjee, to upload video content to a single, developer-owned YouTube channel.

What data this app accesses

This app uses the YouTube Data API's youtube.upload scope solely to upload video files, along with their title and description, to the developer's own YouTube channel. No data belonging to any other user is accessed, collected, or processed.

Data storage

This app does not store, sell, or share any Google user data with third parties. OAuth credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets within the developer's private GitHub repository and are used exclusively to authenticate the automated upload process.

Data sharing

No data collected or accessed by this app is shared with any third party for any purpose, including advertising.
